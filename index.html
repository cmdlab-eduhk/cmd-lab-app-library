About CMD Lab

Original ideas.
Shared experiments.

CMD Lab is a student ensemble at EdUHK. Members compose and perform original music while exploring interactive media, music education, and new performance practices.

This library helps members choose tools for a clear creative or educational purpose—not technology for its own sake.

Keep it useful

Contribute an app

Suggest a tool that supports composition, teaching, interactive work, recording, or performance. Include a concise use case and a member who can help others get started.
